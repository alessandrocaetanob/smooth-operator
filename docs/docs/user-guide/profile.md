# Profile

You can manage your personal information and password from the **Profile** page.

## Opening your profile

Click your avatar or name in the top-right corner of the app, then select **Profile** — or navigate directly to `/profile`.

## Display name

Your display name appears in the app header, on connection activity logs, and in admin views. Click the name field to edit it and save.

## Avatar

Smooth Operator supports profile avatars.

- Click **Change Avatar** and choose an image file (JPEG, PNG, WebP — max 2 MB)
- Your avatar is displayed in the app header and in user management views
- To remove your avatar, click **Remove Avatar**; the system will use your initials instead

## Password

To change your password:

1. Scroll to the **Change Password** section
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter your new password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Confirm the new password
5. Click **Update Password**

:::note
If you signed up via SSO (OIDC or SAML), your password is managed by your identity provider. The password change section won't be available.
:::

## Forgot your password?

If you can't log in, use the **Forgot password?** link on the login page. An email with a reset link will be sent to your registered address — provided your admin has configured SMTP.

## Account deactivation

You cannot deactivate your own account. Only an Admin or Owner can activate or deactivate user accounts. Contact your system administrator if you need your account removed.
